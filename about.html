How We Started

Founded with a passion for quality, The Bloemfontein Coffee Roasting Co. started as a small local setup dedicated to perfecting the roastingprocess. Today, we serve daily commuters, enthusiats and local businesses.

Ethical Sourcing & Quality

We work directly with sustainable coffee farms across Africa and South America, ensuring fair trade practices while sourcing high-grade specialty beans.
